How settlement works. Payout tables above are computed from live on-chain positions using the same parimutuel formula the contract runs: your_stake ÷ winning_pool × total_pool. Calling claim executes that math on-chain and the contract emits a GEN transfer message to the winner via emit_transfer(value=…, on=…). These emitted transfers settle at network finalization. On the Bradbury testnet, transactions have been observed holding at ACCEPTED without progressing to FINALIZED, so a payout can be correctly computed, executed and recorded on-chain while the balance credit remains pending at the network layer — verified across multiple contracts with both on='accepted' and on='finalized'. Escrow deposits settle normally. Every figure on this page is derived from live chain state, never from a database.
